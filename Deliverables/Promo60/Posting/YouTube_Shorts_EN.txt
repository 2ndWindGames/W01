VioletTap is a neon arcade game about quick reactions and accurate taps.

Reach a 10-hit combo for 2× points, then 50 for 3×. Trigger Fever to take on more targets at once.
Grab hourglasses for extra time, avoid bombs, and stay sharp as targets disappear faster. When the numbers appear, tap 1 → 2 → 3 in order!
