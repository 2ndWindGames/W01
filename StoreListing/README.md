# Violet Tap / 네온터치 Store Listing Package

## Contents

- `ko-KR/store-listing.md`: Korean store copy and release notes
- `en-US/store-listing.md`: English store copy and release notes
- `common/feature_graphic_*.png`: Korean/English Google Play feature graphics (1024 × 500)
- `mockups`: promotional layout drafts only; these are not gameplay captures and must not be uploaded as store screenshots
- `en-US/screenshots` and `ko-KR/screenshots`: four real Unity Game View captures per language at 1440 × 2960: intro, ready, gameplay, sound
- `common/contact_sheet_*.png`: four-up previews only; upload the individual portrait captures instead
- `common/feature_graphic_*.png`: 1024 × 500 promotional key art with exact localized titles, not a gameplay capture
- `source/feature_energy_background.png`: text-free generated feature illustration
- `../Tools/BuildCapturedStoreAssets.ps1`: reproduces the screenshots, previews, and feature graphics

## Upload notes

- Upload each language's copy and screenshots to its matching Google Play custom store listing language.
- Do not upload files under `mockups` as gameplay screenshots.
- Store screenshots must show the actual shipped UI and gameplay state.
- Confirm the final package name, app icon, privacy policy, Data safety form, content rating, ads declaration, and in-app product status in Play Console before rollout.
