## Short description

## Full description

Conquer the neon arena with quick decisions and faster fingers!

Violet Tap is a fast-paced reflex game where every signal matters. Hit normal targets to score, keep your streak alive to trigger Fever Mode, and react to special targets before they disappear. Time bonuses extend the round, while danger targets can break your momentum.

Key features

- Quick, intense arcade rounds
- Normal, quick, time-bonus, and danger targets with distinct rules
- Streak and Fever systems that reward accurate play
- Nickname registration when you set a new personal best
- Global rankings for competing with other players
- Separate BGM and sound-effect controls
- Optional one-time in-app purchase to remove ads

How fast and accurate are you? Enter the arena and set a new record in Violet Tap.
